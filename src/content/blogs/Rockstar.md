It all depends on the way one perceives things. But Rockstar is yet a deep and philosophical movie. Starting from the beginning till the end, it tried to delivered certain messages which I felt and would like to share the same.

## Rockstar isn’t just a film, it’s a way of living.

Nobody cares until you are successful. Even if you do the good, people won’t value it until you reach a position. Relatable scene: Just like nobody valued JJ when he used to sing on the bus stand.
JJ was accused of false charges, he was blamed for robbery in his house and was thrown out which lead him to Dargah . And thus his true journey started from there. There he got his identity. He realised the truth of life there only. He got his motivation and the offers from there only. This is the best lesson of the movie -His situations led him to Dargah, where he got his identity and people accepted him the way he was (unlike before) When nobody values you, God is still there. Just remember things are way better then you plan.
When things go tough, it comes out Real. Just like the Sadda Haq song which protrayed the truth and freedom, JJ seeked and wanted others to seek. The pain he had got reflected at the end and people loved it, because then his songs came from his soul.
Lots of pain and sacrifices are required to be really successful in life.Glory comes from pain. JJ had to leave his home, his family, his friends, love and find his own true path. Giving time to himself made him analysed his life and reach his destiny.
All his life JJ wanted to be Rockstar, but in the initials he didn’t had any such story or mark of a Rockstar. But later in his life, the isolation, the pain, the heartbreak, the criticism moulded his journey and made him indeed a Rockstar. So believing in yourself is indeed important. Just trust the journey you are going through, for it is to make you the Real YOU. May be in the initials it’s tough for you, but the pain you have, the struggle you faces will only lead you to the destined version of you.
The human desires are never constant. All his life JJ wanted to be a Rockstar. But later Heer became his obsession. He started not valuing the things he actually wanted in his life because his needs got changed. But later he became the real Rockstar which he was destined to be. So situations and things just happen to make you reach the destined you.
And lastly, there is that special person who can make you go crazy anytime. Who become your strengths and weakness both. This is the person with whom you can be the real you. So value them until you are together.
